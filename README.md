# Benjamin L. Stilwell

Mechanical engineer and U.S. Army veteran in Friendswood, Texas. Twenty-five years of hands-on work, from construction crews and Army systems administration to engineering research, now focused on instrumentation, data acquisition and machine learning for scientific imaging. I build tools, test them against measured ground truth, and report what holds up.

## Current work
- **Vesuvius Challenge (Herculaneum scrolls).** Open-source tools for reading carbonized papyrus scrolls from X-ray CT scans:
  - streaming terabyte-scale CT volumes (OME-Zarr) a piece at a time on a single workstation;
  - segmenting the rolled papyrus into its layers with voxel-affinity networks, connectome-style supervoxels and agglomeration;
  - tracing each layer's turn of the spiral and flattening it into a readable page;
  - detecting ink with 3-D convolutional networks, scored on regions held out from training.
- **Combinatorial packing optimization.** Reinforcement learning with physics-based relaxation for packing problems (Kaggle).
- **Instrumentation and data acquisition.** Modular data-acquisition systems and their software interfaces.
